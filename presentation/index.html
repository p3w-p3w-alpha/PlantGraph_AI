What this work demonstrates.

Real design source

Lower Fox River Water Treatment Plant Operation & Maintenance Plan, Tetra Tech EC, hosted by EPA. Normal-operation drawing P107, PDF page101; §§6.5,6.6,7.5,7.7 and8.

Open the original EPA document ↗

The3D layout is illustrative. Missing curves, boundary heads, pipe properties, elevations and control gains are documented assumptions. Contractor drawings retain their own rights.

Computed observations

EPANET2.2 through WNTR1.5 computes the hydraulic state. Tank inventory uses mass balance. No BATADAL or unrelated readings are assigned to either station. Neither case is a replay of a recorded utility incident or a field-calibrated twin.

Actual Gemma evidence

gemma-4-26b-a4b-it through the Gemini API was used for real image extraction and Atlas investigation. The extraction and investigation screenshots in this presentation are verified Gemma captures. No Claude screenshots are included.

The uninterrupted Lower Fox Gemma investigation test encountered Google HTTP429. Reduced context and bounded retries were implemented, but a successful full rerun is not claimed.

Validation

92 backend tests passed. The full production browser run passed22 of23 checks; the remaining failure was the Lower Fox quota response. Mesh selection, focus/orbit/zoom, branch flow, tank geometry, case isolation and detached comparisons passed.
